## MA_INRA_CEREALS

- Source: https://www.inra.org.ma/sites/default/files/publications/ouvrages/guideorgefr.pdf
- Publisher: INRA Morocco / KAFACI
- Date evidence: INRA bibliographic catalog identifies the guide as 2014; PDF creation date also falls in 2014.
- Geographic scope: Morocco; distinguish bour (rainfed) from irrigated systems.
- Retrieval use: Historical crop guide. Rates depend on yield target, soil supply, water regime, and local conditions. Do not convert its nutrient/product units automatically.
- License metadata: No explicit open-reuse license verified; source attribution retained.

## MA_INRA_OLIVE

- Source: https://www.inra.org.ma/sites/default/files/publications/ouvrages/olivierkafacifr.pdf
- Publisher: INRA Morocco / KAFACI
- Date evidence: Printed imprint DIC - INRA Edition - 2016 on PDF page 33, visually verified.
- Geographic scope: Moroccan olive-growing conditions; distinguish rainfed, supplementary-irrigated, young, and bearing orchards.
- Retrieval use: Historical guide. Orchard age, production, irrigation, soil tests, and foliar analysis matter. Do not transfer per-tree values into per-hectare doses without verified assumptions.
- License metadata: No explicit open-reuse license verified; source attribution retained.

## MA_PNTTA_DOUKKALA_70

- Source: https://www.agrimaroc.net/bulletins/btta_70.pdf
- Publisher: Moroccan Ministry of Agriculture / PNTTA; bulletin produced at IAV Hassan II; hosted by Agrimaroc.net
- Date evidence: July 2000 printed on bulletin 70, PDF page 1.
- Geographic scope: Bas-Service irrigated perimeter of Doukkala; do not generalize to all Moroccan soils or to rainfed fields.
- Retrieval use: Regional historical calibration reference, relevant background to the Doukkala imagery area. It is not evidence of the soil test results, crop identity, or nutrient status of the pictured parcels.

## MA_SOIL_MAPS_2025

- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05699-x.pdf
- Publisher: Scientific Data / Springer Nature; Bouslihim et al., including INRA Morocco researchers
- Date evidence: Canonical publisher article page gives publication date 8 August 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s41597-025-05699-x.
- Geographic scope: National Moroccan cropland maps at 250 m resolution, based on samples collected in 2010-2022.
- Retrieval use: National baseline predictions are not parcel-specific measurements. No geospatial map rasters or underlying private Fertimap samples were downloaded. Do not infer a pictured field value from a figure in the paper.
- License metadata: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
